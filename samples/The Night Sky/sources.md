# Sources and facts to check

- Dark adaptation: roughly 20–30 minutes for the eye to reach full night sensitivity (rods).
- Venus is usually the first "star" of evening — it is a planet.
- *Planet* from Greek *planētēs*, "wanderer".
- The IAU recognises 88 constellations (1922; boundaries fixed 1930).
- Light from the Sun takes about 8 minutes 20 seconds to reach Earth.
- Andromeda Galaxy: about 2.5 million light-years away; visible to the naked eye under dark skies.
