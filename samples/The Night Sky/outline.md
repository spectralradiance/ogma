# The Night Sky — outline

A short essay in four sections, moving outward: from the first light of evening, to the patterns we draw, to the planets that refuse the patterns, to the age of the light itself.

1. **First Light** — dusk, the first star, the eye adjusting
2. **Constellations** — pattern-making; the same stars, different stories
3. **The Wandering Stars** — planets; the word *planet* means wanderer
4. **Starlight and Time** — every star seen as it was, not as it is

Tone: plain, unhurried, a little wonder. No lists in the finished text.
