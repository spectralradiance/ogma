## Starlight and Time

Light is fast, but it is not instant. Sunlight is already eight minutes old by the time it reaches us, and the light of the stars is older still — years old, centuries old, sometimes far more.

So every star is seen as it was, not as it is. On a dark night the Andromeda Galaxy is just visible as a faint smudge, and the light that makes it left there two and a half million years ago, long before there was anyone here to see it.

To look up, then, is to look back. The night sky is the oldest thing most of us will ever see.
